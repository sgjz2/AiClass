## Build a concept for this brief

Choose a direction from the audience, usage situation, main reason to care or brand character. Develop it through product pose, scale, space, color and light. Lifestyle settings, abstract spaces, graphic compositions and conceptual suspension can all work. A background may suggest use or simply provide mood and beauty.

Serve the current core product value and communication goal. Emphasize functional benefits, experience, emotional value or brand character with factual claims grounded in evidence. Atmosphere and ornament may create attraction without each representing a product function. Choose an expression the target audience can recognize and find interesting, rather than reusing a test case's setting, props or arrangement.

Identify a concrete relationship: a product silhouette echoed by its surroundings, an action establishing scale, or contrasting materials. Develop the remaining elements around that relationship. A brand series may retain a shared visual language.

When the direction is open, compare a few meaningfully different concepts if useful. When a direction is chosen, develop its successful relationships. Judge theme and composition together.

## Default composition preferences

Keep the product the main subject. Its actual visible mass should be clearly larger than the complete headline group; compare silhouettes rather than transparent margins or whitespace. Set scale by product shape, pose and placement, retaining both recognition and expressive type. After scale checks, review actual visual weight as well.

Prefer an edge-to-edge scene or graphic background, integrating copy into readable areas. Integrate supporting information into areas suited to reading.

Avoid a complete person unless the task needs one; prefer relevant crops or actions when people help explain use. Keep the product primary: human area, clarity, faces and gaze must not take its focus. Design cropping together with the viewpoint: check operator/product/viewer positions, arm origins, action target and contact. Retain the context needed for the action to make spatial sense. Before removing a person, check whether the pose still works. Actions dependent on an operator need a coherent crop or changed viewpoint rather than arms without an intelligible origin or actions without an operator.

Follow an explicit brief requesting type-led, person-led or other special composition instead of these defaults.

## Arrange reading priorities

### Judge actual visual weight

Set the intended order of product, full headline group, transaction group and supporting elements. Promotions may lead with confirmed prices while preserving product presence. Compare actual area, tonal/color contrast, weight/stroke density, detail, position, surrounding negative space and high-contrast backing. Assess whole multi-line groups, enclosure marks and price shapes, not isolated characters or bounding boxes; avoid false precision and universal percentages.

A large pale product may still lose to dense dark type; large fine type behind a subject may remain secondary. Check [weight diagnosis](visual-examples.md#visual-weight-diagnosis). Inspect the first attention point and subsequent groups at full/display size. This is design review rather than eye tracking or conversion measurement. Locate competition before changing secondary weight, width/spacing, backing contrast, price/ornament placement, contour or scale. Retain expressive titles and required readability rather than uniformly shrinking type, enlarging products or lowering saturation.

Assign information roles: attract attention, support understanding, and enable action. Set scale, spacing and grouping from required copy and display size. Benefits may use lettering, graphics or icons; equally important facts may be peers.

Confirmed prices and conditions can lead a promotion; brand artwork uses action information as needed. In a requested placeholder draft, normally keep unknown placeholders subordinate or in delivery notes. Follow an explicitly chosen placeholder layout.

Arrange the main visual and message first, then integrate supporting facts, terms and ornament. Judge hierarchy through the actual reading sequence.

## Build design through relationships

Start with the most distinctive relationship: product curves echoing type, a geometric body contrasting soft material, or foreground product interleaving with background information. Support it with color, light and ornament. Open directions may compare genuinely different concepts, not just gradient colors or title positions; develop a chosen direction directly.

Organize reading through alignment, negative-space shapes and density. Symmetry, asymmetry, tilting, out-of-frame elements, enlarged details and overlap can work when direction and hierarchy are visible. A product/brand-derived main color may connect the image, support colors hold space, and emphasis concentrates attention instead of highlighting every region.

At thumbnail size check product/message focus, then assess rewarding detail close up. If it remains a product plus copy, adjust pose, image/type distance, proportion, type rhythm or background form. For competition, reduce secondary area/contrast. Establish relationships before adding ornament while retaining intentional tension.

## Information position and type size

Hierarchy governs order; product contour, scene negative space, semantic links and viewing direction govern position. Top, side, bottom, surrounding and interleaved groups can work, including full-width lower information zones. Avoid repeatedly piling everything above the product without a concept, or scattering related copy merely for variety.

| Role | Position | Scale/emphasis |
|---|---|---|
| Main heading/core benefit | Adjacent, diagonal or enclosing relation to product, in any suitable region | Main text focus; judge the whole group rather than equally enlarging every name/slogan |
| Brand/name/subtitle | Identity or explanatory group near title/product | Distinguish by recognition needs; familiarity, long names and brief can change size |
| Benefits/specifications | Group together or near the explained product area | Subordinate to primary text; peers share emphasis, keywords/numerals may stand out |
| Price/offer/CTA | One transaction group including currency/conditions, on a side or lower area | Promotions may give price title-level weight; separate value/unit/support, keeping CTA clear |
| Dates/conditions/support | Near the statement they qualify | Quieter but readable at display size; critical conditions remain accessible |

Size follows actual glyph height and viewing scale, not fixed pixels/ratios. A tentative layout can start with main text at 1, subtitle/key benefit at 0.4–0.6 and support at 0.25–0.4, then adjust for length, weight, language and display readability. These are starting points, not acceptance thresholds; prices follow the task. Several small lines can outweigh a large line; dense Chinese glyphs and long English phrases need separate fitting.

At mobile size identify first read, then benefit/price/action and necessary small text. Adjust position, grouping, size, weight and contrast together. Information-rich briefs can use regions rather than all content at the top. A1-01, T1-05, T1-06, G2-07 and T1-04 demonstrate different positions/roles, not reusable claims or one mandatory layout.

## Foreground perspective and contextual framing

For portability, gripping or operation, a product nearer the lens and a receding person can create scale. Establish viewer/operator/product positions, wrist connections, target and occlusion. Keep identity-critical surfaces legible. Strong perspective is not a requirement to bend the product.

Distinguish low viewpoint, wide-angle near/far scale, circular framing and fisheye curvature. A source title cannot establish lens settings. For fisheye expression check continuous scene curvature and accurate construction. Container viewpoints, crossing circular frames and floor perspective can establish depth when appropriate.

Service ads led by human emotion, launch teasers and single-product sales have different priorities. Reference crops cannot override product/people requirements, and a preference for fewer complete people must retain necessary action context. See [P1 references](visual-examples.md#foreground-perspective-and-action-cases).

## Express the theme through type

Preserve exact copy and let letterforms, semantic emphasis and arrangement participate in the composition. Ordinary type, custom lettering, whole-line contrast, word or character emphasis, varied scale, orientation and asymmetry are options. Choose variation from the theme and reading rhythm; uniform forms with deliberate spacing and proportion may also create tension.

Product dominance governs overall hierarchy; it does not require every headline to be small, light, compact or low contrast. Retain expressive character appropriate to the subject. Uniform type still needs deliberate relationships through proportion, spacing or other choices. Accurate reading is a baseline; orderly transcription alone does not establish thematic character or designed rhythm.

Check natural sentence reading, semantic emphasis, clear glyphs, and the relationship to product pose, background and brand character. Choose fonts, line counts, weight order and amount of variation for this design.

## Compose with ornament

### Establish a shared type and ornament character

After priorities, choose compatible relationships among title forms, semantic emphasis, prices/numerals, support and ornament. Specify width, stroke contrast/terminals, phrase rhythm/tilt, line weight/curvature/ends, material, color and depth. Prestige adjectives or shared color alone do not describe this relationship.

Coordination does not require one font, weight or line type. Whole-line contrast, semantic emphasis, heavy braces with fine arcs or calm lettering with broad curves can work through direction, color, scale, enclosure or depth. Avoid making every block a separate emphasis or prescribing one reference's light/heavy pair, arc or brush treatment.

Design price, CTA and icons with the title. Check whether serif headings, speed lettering, brush-price backing, round badges and dimensional ribbons each compete without a common relationship. Repeated backing/badges/contrast must not restore support to first focus. Pure ornament may create mood/beauty without representing function. Review text accuracy, phrase rhythm, ornament relationship and product hierarchy separately. Repair local line weight, curvature, ends, spacing, color or grouping while retaining the effective scene/product; see [diagnosis](visual-examples.md#visual-weight-diagnosis).

Lines, brackets, corners, loops, free strokes, doodles, shapes, dimensional accents or no ornament are available choices. Decoration may organize information or simply create mood, beauty, rhythm or intentional contrast.

Pure ornament does not require a functional explanation and must not be removed merely for lacking an information role. Ribbons are one option; choose line forms and materials for this concept rather than making them the default across subjects.

Coordinate color, scale, stroke weight, curvature, position, density and whitespace. Open ends, breaks and interweaving can work. Choose decorative materials for the concept. Correct accidental tangencies, unreadable required copy or impaired product recognition locally.

Judge purely decorative marks by overall coherence. Check informational leaders for a clear target, and functional or structural diagrams against product evidence.

## Refine type and ornament details

Refine optical alignment, letter spacing, line spacing, keyword proportions and punctuation first, linking the word group with product contour or action. Break by meaning and keep models, numbers and units associated; tidy bounding boxes alone do not establish optical alignment.

Design endpoints, corners, weight, overlap and whitespace deliberately. Choose tags, leaders and supporting graphics by content rather than adding invented parameters, badges, meaningless small text or icon rows to create detail. Fine lines and small copy must remain recognizable at display size. Adjust scale or reduce secondary content instead of using sharpening to compensate.

## Translate into image instructions

Specify the theme, exact wording, overall character and key image/type relationships, leaving letterforms, emphasis, layout and ornament room to develop together. Fix user-selected treatments and decisions necessary to the concept.

Carry explicit prohibitions and product boundaries into instructions without adding unrequested conservative styles such as a uniform font, fixed two-line weight order, a single thin straight rule or low contrast. Continue established expression in local edits. Do not remove all effective lettering, ornament and contrast to reduce an AI appearance; address the actual issue.

Retain correct, effective generated lettering. Local edits should continue established forms, texture and composition. Select precise text tools when correction reliability or editable delivery requires them.

Judge actual reading rhythm and coherence. Keep effective alternatives while meeting explicit user requirements.

## Large background lettering and parameters

Large type can recede through reduced contrast, overlap and depth of field. Large size is not automatically reading priority. Apply the product-led requirement to the whole main headline group; renaming it decoration does not bypass hierarchy. If a required model/value/unit is overlapped, show it completely in a readable region as well. Operational arrows and length settings must match confirmed construction. See [L1-05, P1-02 and P1-06](visual-examples.md) as optional relationships, not templates.

## Typography lessons from the benchmarks

L1-02–L1-04 create force through clear title/model/support roles and spacing, without complex lettering. L1-01/L1-05 move large letters behind the subject through overlap and contrast. P1-02 connects action and keywords with local color; P1-06 lets confirmed parameters inhabit space. Select one main relationship for this message instead of stacking every technique.

Set headline semantic focus, contour and product clearance before adding icons, price labels or action buttons. With permission to shorten copy, merge related functions into concise support. Accommodate required information through hierarchy rather than silently removing it. Huge headings, rows of icons and large buttons are optional ecommerce choices.
