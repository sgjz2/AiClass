# Typography and ornament

Purpose: develop headline forms, copy composition and decorative relationships. Read when generating or refining image text. Use [production and review](production.md) for execution and [reference use](reference-guide.md) for library selection.

For optional lettering and ornament examples, see [visual forms](visual-form-reference.md#typography-and-ornament).

## Express the theme through type

Preserve exact copy and let letterforms, semantic emphasis and arrangement participate in the composition. Ordinary type, custom lettering, whole-line contrast, word or character emphasis, varied scale, orientation and asymmetry are options. Choose variation from the theme and reading rhythm; uniform forms with deliberate spacing and proportion may also create tension.

Product dominance governs overall hierarchy; it does not require every headline to be small, light, compact or low contrast. Retain expressive character appropriate to the subject. Uniform type still needs deliberate relationships through proportion, spacing or other choices. Accurate reading is a baseline; orderly transcription alone does not establish thematic character or designed rhythm.

Check natural sentence reading, semantic emphasis, clear glyphs, and the relationship to product pose, background and brand character. Choose fonts, line counts, weight order and amount of variation for this design.

## Compose with ornament

Lines, brackets, corners, loops, free strokes, doodles, shapes, dimensional accents or no ornament are available choices. Decoration may organize information or simply create mood, beauty, rhythm or intentional contrast.

Pure ornament does not require a functional explanation and must not be removed merely for lacking an information role. Ribbons are one option; choose line forms and materials for this concept rather than making them the default across subjects.

Coordinate color, scale, stroke weight, curvature, position, density and whitespace. Open ends, breaks and interweaving can work. Choose decorative materials for the concept. Correct accidental tangencies, unreadable required copy or impaired product recognition locally.

Judge purely decorative marks by overall coherence. Check informational leaders for a clear target, and functional or structural diagrams against product evidence.

## Refine type and ornament details

Refine optical alignment, letter spacing, line spacing, keyword proportions and punctuation first, linking the word group with product contour or action. Break by meaning and keep models, numbers and units associated; tidy bounding boxes alone do not establish optical alignment.

Design endpoints, corners, weight, overlap and whitespace deliberately. Choose tags, leaders and supporting graphics by content rather than adding invented parameters, badges, meaningless small text or icon rows to create detail. Fine lines and small copy must remain recognizable at display size. Adjust scale or reduce secondary content instead of using sharpening to compensate.

## Translate into image instructions

Specify the theme, exact wording, overall character and key image/type relationships, leaving letterforms, emphasis, layout and ornament room to develop together. Fix user-selected treatments and decisions necessary to the concept.

Carry explicit prohibitions and product boundaries into instructions without adding unrequested conservative styles such as a uniform font, fixed two-line weight order, a single thin straight rule or low contrast. Continue established expression in local edits. Do not remove all effective lettering, ornament and contrast to reduce an AI appearance; address the actual issue.

Retain correct, effective generated lettering. Local edits should continue established forms, texture and composition. Select precise text tools when correction reliability or editable delivery requires them.

Judge actual reading rhythm and coherence. Keep effective alternatives while meeting explicit user requirements.

## Large background lettering and parameters

Large type can recede through reduced contrast, overlap and depth of field. Large size is not automatically reading priority. Apply the product-led requirement to the whole main headline group; renaming it decoration does not bypass hierarchy. If a required model/value/unit is overlapped, show it completely in a readable region as well. Operational arrows and length settings must match confirmed construction. See [L1-05, P1-02 and P1-06](lighting-and-perspective-study.md) as optional relationships, not templates.

## Typography lessons from the benchmarks

L1-02–L1-04 create force through clear title/model/support roles and spacing, without complex lettering. L1-01/L1-05 move large letters behind the subject through overlap and contrast. P1-02 connects action and keywords with local color; P1-06 lets confirmed parameters inhabit space. Select one main relationship for this message instead of stacking every technique.

Set headline semantic focus, contour and product clearance before adding icons, price labels or action buttons. With permission to shorten copy, merge related functions into concise support. Accommodate required information through hierarchy rather than silently removing it. Huge headings, rows of icons and large buttons are optional ecommerce choices.
