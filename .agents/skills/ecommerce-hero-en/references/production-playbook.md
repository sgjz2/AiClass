# Production, repair and delivery

Record the actual placement, audience, identity constraints, main message and evidence, exact copy/terms, language/count and dimensions. Use [visual development](art-direction.md) to develop the task/background relationship and brief-appropriate information hierarchy, distinguish confirmed offers from draft placeholders, and judge selective detail in the actual export. Select and open a small coherent subset of local references. Connect reference IDs to observed mechanisms, prompt decisions and excluded content; records do not substitute for viewing.

Plan the complete product/scene/type relationship before choosing unified generation, authentic product composition or generated lettering/decoration layers. Follow [generative typography](generative-typography.md). Expressive type may compose with the product while retaining its character; this project defaults to product dominance, assessed through [unified judgment](typography-and-ornament.md). Graphic environments, prominent headings, strong promotions and credible conceptual suspension are valid routes; pale photography is not universal.

For rendering-related work, use [light and material judgment](render-and-material.md) in both prompting and actual inspection: light describes volume, confirmed surfaces keep suitable responses, highlights and detail have compositional roles. Refine specific competing areas while preserving character; soft light or desaturation is not a default repair.

Include identity invariants, input roles, exact words/terms and key lighting and composition relationships, leaving type and ornament choices open under [unified judgment](typography-and-ornament.md); fix user-selected treatments and necessary concept decisions. Only a background-only route excludes all text; a scene-plus-generated-copy route must include it. Size authentic layers by visible content rather than transparent margins, matching angle and lighting. References do not authorize gifts, accessories, numbers or claims.

For realistic work, inspect every support point, perspective, light, reflections, edges, depth and foreground occlusion. For conceptual work, inspect its own spatial rules. A single ellipse or unchanged source pixels do not establish integration. Change scene/source or edit locally if necessary; do not hide bad integration with text.

Save a baseline and concrete differences. Repair spelling locally first; use style-matched precise overlays when generation cannot resolve the error. Do not flatten good lettering to system fonts merely for editability. Unstable identity requires authentic assets. Preserve useful tension when reducing artificial effects.

Identity and factual errors block delivery; finish is assessed separately. Open actual exports at full and intended/mobile size, checking every product detail, required word, term, crop, format and actual dimensions. Product/core value, intentional typography, clear commercial groups and legible copy must work together. A brand KV does not automatically need a price or CTA; a promotion brief requiring price/dates does.

Negative references require different repairs: G1-01 competing lead messages, G1-02 product integration and offer/type grouping, G1-03 a product detached inside a sidebar. Do not remove mandatory conditions or replace all problems with smaller text. Compare observed mechanisms, never average away a failure or present self-review as conversion evidence.

Deliver real artwork, actual specifications, inputs/prompts/edit history, baseline/important changes, full-size and small-size review and gaps. Composition includes source/configuration; unified generation includes original outputs and reproducibility materials without fabricated editable layers or guaranteed pixel replay. Check reference/font availability when packaging. Verify exact dimensions rather than claiming them from an approximate generated aspect.


## 文字设计落实 / Typography implementation

文字反馈按[文字研究](typography-reference-study.md)提取适用关系，不把观察变成逐项固定动作。导出复查准确全文、主题表达、阅读节奏与整体协调；成立的替代表达可以保留，具体用户要求仍须满足。场景有效而字平庸时，保留基线局部修字。参考底带、品牌、额外句和生成细节不自动迁移。Inspect actual typography, not just spelling; do not claim improvement without a new inspected output.
