# Visual development and finishing

Describe a visible relationship rather than adjectives: which action, scale, spatial, material or letterform relationship communicates which evidenced main message? Inspect real references by role: identity, composition, light, type/decoration or commercial groups. Transfer mechanisms across categories without copying claims or props. Respect the user’s explicit positive/negative labels in the index; an isolated useful mechanism cannot turn a negative image into a quality benchmark.

Specify product scale, camera angle, overlap, reading path and support/conceptual suspension; key light and material response; letterform character and relationship to product direction; main/supporting/transaction groups and terms; the integrated type/ornament relationship using [unified rules](typography-and-ornament.md), including atmosphere and beauty as legitimate roles. Rich information can be organized. Many equally strong focal points require restructuring. Gentle light and small type are not universal defaults.

Examples from the atlas: G5-04 uses a strong offer heading while products remain identifiable; G6-04 demonstrates an action; G7-07 separates dark material through light and mesh; G2-06 uses large type and shoe overlap in a graphic environment. Select mechanisms instead of stacking effects.

Compare a few expressive mechanisms when direction is open; retain a successful baseline when it is chosen. Develop type, scene and product together with [generative typography](generative-typography.md), then inspect identity and facts. Authentic product layers require real integration work; dense copy does not automatically require a text-free image with plain-font overlays.

For pasted-on integration, render development or overprocessed appearance, follow [light and material judgment](render-and-material.md). Inspect actual references for volume-building light, material responses, highlight/detail hierarchy and quiet areas. Glossy surfaces, saturated color and dramatic lighting remain possible. Match the environment when exact source pixels matter; if relighting is allowed, verify identity rather than re-overlaying a mismatched old cutout. Bland work may need stronger communication, type, scale, material or offers; diagnose overprocessing locally and preserve useful expression.

Local repairs preserve good layout, lettering, identity and accurate copy. Edit a spelling error locally before resorting to a style-matched overlay. Rebuild only when the concept or composition fails, rather than randomly regenerating the whole image.

Resolve identity/fact blockers, then review theme, hierarchy, type/decoration, transactions, space and materials. Realistic work needs correct contacts and shadows; conceptual suspension needs coherent projection and overlap. Full-size inspection covers detail, small-size inspection covers focal hierarchy and readability. Also judge actual highlight hierarchy, material distinction and texture strength under the shared rendering reference; correct words and support do not establish render quality. Brand KVs need not have prices/buttons; promotions must retain required terms.

Compare actual reference mechanisms with concrete gaps. The atlas's G1 examples distinguish clutter, pasted-on integration and detached product scale. Pixel fidelity is not visual quality and generated atmosphere is not performance evidence. Previous case runs are experience, not controlled skill comparisons or conversion data.
