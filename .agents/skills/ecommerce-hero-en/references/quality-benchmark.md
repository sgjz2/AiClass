## Establish visible targets before production

Unless reference viewing is explicitly prohibited, actually open 1–3 suitable originals from these 12 before a new poster or major rebuild. Usually one guides the main composition and another supplements lighting or typography. Local refinement can continue using already-viewed references without reloading every round. Originals ship with the skill; resolve paths using [reference use](reference-guide.md). Reading analysis alone does not count as viewing an original.

Record briefly: `reference ID → relationship worth adapting → how this product implements it → where to check it`. Identify the main presentation face, viewpoint, light/dark focus and how type responds to the product, then produce. If direction is open, compare two conceptual relationships and develop one; extra image generations or a user selection are not required just to compare concepts.

## Shared criteria

| Dimension | Visible target | Return for revision when |
|---|---|---|
| Main visual relationship | A dominant visible relationship: product overlaps background lettering, action and foreground product share a diagonal, or one hue gains material/tonal depth | Only an attractive room, a product and copy remain; any unrelated product could replace it without changing composition |
| Product expression | Face, scale and viewpoint emphasize silhouette, material or use with clear visual weight | File size alone makes it nominally primary while faces, furniture, titles or ornament take attention |
| Light and materials | Highlights describe curvature/turns; fabric, plastic, glass and metal respond appropriately; necessary dark structures remain legible | Uniform contour tracing, washed-out scenes, metallic plastic, pasted-on products or unrelated shadows |
| Space and action | Support, overlap, perspective and conceptual action read coherently across foreground, middle and background | Background blur substitutes for space; arms lack origins, contact fails or products deform mechanically |
| Type and information | Main message precedes support; proportions/spacing respond to silhouette, movement or negative space | Brand, title, parameters and price all compete; every region is equally filled; icon/button rows fill unused space |
| Color and finish | Shared dominant color with tonal/material depth, concentrated contrast and clean full-size edges, marks and type | Filters replace color design, light has no source, fragmented detail, or a busy thumbnail hides rough full-size output |

Different briefs can meet these criteria differently. Fixed area ratios, aggregate scores and prestige adjectives do not replace image judgment. Low-resolution reference text, dense small copy or teaser darkness are not defects to reproduce.

## Translate methods into actual instructions

Describe the selected relationship rather than only asking for flagship, dramatic or commercial quality, or naming a brand. Leave other design choices open.

- Lighting example: broad soft light turns across the real shell in quiet dark space, a narrower reflection separates the silhouette, contact shadow establishes weight, the brand area remains clear, and background lettering recedes. Ground materials in the actual product.
- Perspective example: bring the camera close to the foreground product while preserving its confirmed presentation face. Let the person/scene recede; preserve wrist connection and operation direction. Arrange the title in action-created negative space, away from marks.
- Shared-hue example: separate dark background, lit product surfaces and different roughness within one color family. Secondary views support the main view; confirmed prices/specifications form a distinct supporting layer.

Rebuild relationships for a new aspect ratio. A vertical stack may become diagonal or centrally focused in a horizontal image; do not squeeze the reference or default to two equal columns. Identity images establish the product; style images guide design relationships. Label roles when passing references to the image tool. Never copy reference brands, claims, identities or products into the current product.

## Compare and revise before delivery

Compare the actual export and selected original at similar viewing sizes. At thumbnail size inspect focus/reading order; at full size inspect materials, edges, construction and text. Record the clearest gap, edit and recheck. Do not claim reference-level quality without actually viewing the reference and checking the export.

After identity, text, price and specification checks, complete visual review. Address a clearly weaker concept or light/space relationship with targeted revision. Rebuild a failed overall relationship; edit local issues locally. After two targeted attempts at the same issue without improvement, reassess the route, retain the best version and describe the unresolved gap. Avoid unlimited micro-edits or calling an unfinished result complete. Self-review is separate from user acceptance and cannot guarantee identical generator performance every time.
