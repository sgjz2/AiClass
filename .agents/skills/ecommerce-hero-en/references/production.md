# Production and review

Purpose: manage explicit constraints, brief, product evidence, production route, local corrections and delivery checks. Read when producing or refining artwork. Use the design references for aesthetic decisions.

## Explicit requirements and prohibitions

Distinguish explicit user requirements, prohibitions and default preferences in the brief, recording each item's scope. Requirements and prohibitions are acceptance constraints; aesthetic freedom, reference forms and document simplification cannot weaken them. Changes require an explicit user update; greater visual appeal is not permission to change them.

Read the current user instructions and the task package's brief, brand standards and constraint records first. Keep product-specific or case-specific prohibitions with those task materials, carry them into the actual instructions and review them. General guidance protects these requirements without extending them to unrelated products.

## Establish the brief and product evidence

Extract communication goal, placement, audience, core product value and its evidence, brand character, required copy/terms, language, quantity, dimensions and format. Translate confirmed product facts into an audience-relevant reason to buy, then establish the main message and its visual expression. The task determines whether prices, campaign details, multiple languages or particular asset types are needed; examples are not input thresholds or output templates. Separate user requirements, supplied facts, design inference and unknowns. Ask together when missing information changes identity, required content or core direction; otherwise make reasonable choices and continue.

Record silhouette, color, patterns, connections, logos/lettering, quantity and accessories. Placement and viewpoint may change, but do not invent construction, parts or included goods absent from source imagery and unconfirmed by materials or the user. Typical category configurations and scene needs are not confirming evidence. Keep unknown regions occluded, change viewpoint, request evidence or use reliable authentic materials.

Props must not create unconfirmed connections, imply included goods or bypass a prohibited presentation. A scene-prop or not-included caption does not exempt these constraints. Inspect the construction implied by actual connections, support and occlusion.

Prices, dates, certifications, sales rankings, performance, gifts and accessories require confirmed evidence. Never invent unknown facts. Prefer authentic logo assets; use supplied brand text when suitable assets are absent, rather than inventing a mark to impersonate the original. Use traceable imagery for measured effects and label generated demonstrations as creative illustrations. Verify official rules when current platform compliance is requested.

Product photos establish identity; finished ads provide design references. Respect restrictions on reference viewing and tool inputs. See [reference use](reference-guide.md) for optional library selection.

## Select the production route

Accurate construction, packaging patterns, marks and required copy are shared acceptance requirements for every method. Strict preservation does not automatically freeze an entire product's source pixels, viewpoint or lighting, or mandate an authentic product layer. Design the overall product/space/light/type relationship, then select or combine methods by available materials, tool capability, specified asset requirements and observed errors:

| Method | Problem it can solve | Adoption/combination checks |
|---|---|---|
| Source-image editing and local generation | Retain effective product/artwork while developing scene, light or local content | Edit scope, identity details and integration across regions |
| Reference-guided unified generation | Product, space, light, type and ornament compose together | Use source assets as identity evidence; compare product details, exact copy and overall relationships |
| Authentic/generated asset composition | Use specified assets or obtain more reliable local corrections | Asset scope, viewpoint, lighting, edges, reflections and support |
| Independent lettering/ornament generation and precise correction | Develop local expression, repair generation errors or provide editable content | Style, texture, perspective, overlap and readability |

One image may combine several methods. Treat accuracy-sensitive regions individually; a local mark or pattern issue does not automatically require replacing the entire product. When the user or brand standards explicitly require source-pixel preservation, record the asset and scope and use authentic assets there. Do not upgrade ordinary identity accuracy to pixel preservation on your own. Generated results still require comparison with source evidence; do not promise unverified exact identity.

Consider generating headings, price lettering, icons, buttons and decoration with the image and retain correct, effective generated type. The chosen methods must jointly achieve product accuracy, marketing expression and visual integration; technical fidelity does not excuse a pasted-on appearance.

Use real image tools for creation/editing and scripts for composition or precise typesetting. Read the [layout contract](layout-contract.md) only when using that helper.

## Write production instructions

Include confirmed facts and exact copy, applicable explicit requirements and prohibitions, theme and key composition relationships, and open aesthetic choices. State relevant structural and presentation prohibitions in the actual tool instructions; generic requests for product fidelity or skill compliance are insufficient. Assign identity, composition, lighting or lettering roles to input images and retain the actual input list.

Implement user-selected treatments and necessary concept decisions, letting the model coordinate remaining typography, ornament and surfaces. Include image text explicitly for full artwork generation; reserve composition areas appropriately for background-only work.

## Refine locally

Save the selected baseline. Briefly record the issue, actual edit and preserved relationships for each meaningful revision, then inspect the new export.

Keep successful product/scene work when editing type. Correct persistent spelling with style-matched precise tools. Locate identity errors, then choose local editing, authentic-asset correction or a change of generation method and inspect integration afterward; do not automatically cover the whole subject with the source asset. Rebuild the whole composition when its concept fails or local edits remain ineffective; on persistent failure, preserve usable work and report the specific gap.

## Review and delivery

Open actual full-size and intended/mobile-size exports and check:

| Check | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Explicit requirements | Applicable requirements and prohibitions are satisfied individually; prop captions do not mask forbidden construction or presentation |
| Identity and facts | Construction, patterns, marks and accessories match evidence; required words, numbers, currency, dates and terms are accurate |
| Marketing expression | Product is recognizable; the main message and audience-relevant value or brand character read clearly; task-required action information is clear |
| Composition and reading | Product leads; headline has specific thematic character, reading rhythm and image relationships; ornament works with the whole; supporting and action information fit the task |
| Material and space | Light, reflection, perspective, occlusion and support/action relationships are coherent; detail suits viewing scale |
| Specifications | Files exist and open; dimensions, crop, format, language and quantity meet the brief |

Continue correcting failures of explicit requirements, prohibitions, identity or required facts. Label unresolved results as concept/incomplete work and list repairs needed; aesthetic quality cannot compensate for a failed acceptance constraint. Assess aesthetics from actual images and record self-review separately from user acceptance.

Assess communication through visible relationships in the artwork. Without audience tests or campaign data, design self-review is not evidence of improved click rates, purchase intent or conversion.

Deliver artwork and concise notes. Retain original outputs, inputs, actual prompts and edit history. Composition includes source/configuration; unified generation retains materials for a new run. Report only checked specifications and completion status.
