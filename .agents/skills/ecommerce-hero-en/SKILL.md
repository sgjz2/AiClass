# English ecommerce hero

Create ecommerce marketing visuals that help the audience understand core product value and support the current click, purchase or brand communication goal. Analyze the brief, organize evidence, develop concepts, produce actual artwork, and evaluate/refine it. Follow the requested placement, language, quantity, direction and methods; inputs and output specifications come from the current task.

Explicit user requirements and prohibitions remain binding throughout continuation and document maintenance until the user explicitly updates them. Record scope, implement them in actual production instructions and check each through [requirements and prohibitions](references/production.md#explicit-requirements-and-prohibitions).

## Workflow

1. Establish goal, audience, core value and its evidence with [production and review](references/production.md). Organize the brief, constraints and exact copy; ground product construction and commercial claims in original assets and confirmed materials.
2. Select and actually view suitable original references using the [quality benchmark](references/quality-benchmark.md). Establish the composition and lighting relationships to achieve. Develop the overall image with [concept and hierarchy](references/art-direction.md), and image text with [type and ornament](references/typography-and-ornament.md). Respect an explicit prohibition on viewing references while retaining quality checks.
3. Select or combine generation, editing and composition according to the concept, tools and asset requirements. Product accuracy applies across routes rather than prescribing a single method; refine existing successful work locally.
4. Open actual exports at full and intended display sizes. Check product, copy and specifications; compare visual focus, materials/light, space and typography against selected references. Correct clear gaps through [comparison and revision](references/quality-benchmark.md#compare-and-revise-before-delivery), then deliver actual artwork and concise notes. Accurate information alone does not establish visual finish.

Keep aesthetic choices open and product/required facts accurate. Composition defaults live in the design reference; explicit current requirements take priority.

## Local visual preferences

Reduce large decorative gradients. Build depth through composition, color fields, real lighting and material contrast while retaining natural shading. Improve actual product/type clarity, develop intentional local details and retain quiet areas. Express design through image/type relationships, proportion, spacing and thematic connections rather than element count. See [composition](references/art-direction.md#build-design-through-relationships), [color and detail](references/render-and-material.md#reduce-decorative-gradients) and [clarity review](references/production.md#clarity-and-output-size).
